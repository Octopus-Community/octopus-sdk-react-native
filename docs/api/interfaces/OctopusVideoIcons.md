# Interface: OctopusVideoIcons

Icons of the video player.

## Properties

### muted?

> `optional` **muted**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

Shown while the sound is off.

---

### notMuted?

> `optional` **notMuted**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

Shown while the sound is on.

---

### pause?

> `optional` **pause**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

Pauses the video.

---

### play?

> `optional` **play**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

Plays the video.

---

### replay?

> `optional` **replay**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

Replays the video once it has ended.
