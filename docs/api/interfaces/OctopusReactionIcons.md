# Interface: OctopusReactionIcons

Reaction images.

**Not tinted**: like screen-state illustrations, reactions are drawn in their original colors on
both platforms, so a full-color image is expected here.

## Properties

### clap?

> `optional` **clap**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

The clapping-hands reaction.

---

### cry?

> `optional` **cry**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

The crying reaction.

---

### heart?

> `optional` **heart**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

The heart reaction (also the "liked" state of the like button).

---

### joy?

> `optional` **joy**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

The tears-of-joy reaction.

---

### mouthOpen?

> `optional` **mouthOpen**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

The open-mouth (surprised) reaction.

---

### rage?

> `optional` **rage**: `ImageResolvedAssetSource`

The angry reaction.
